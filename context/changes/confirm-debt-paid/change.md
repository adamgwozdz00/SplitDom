## Notes

Roadmap item S-08 (FR-009), GitHub issue #13. The recipient of a payment (S-07's ledger entry) confirms it, which makes it posted and permanent, or rejects it, which keeps it in the ledger as rejected and outside both balances. Also drops FR-005's paid-debt lock of an expense.
